Build-Your-Own-Kuiper.xlsx turns whole numbers into positions with four spreadsheet formulas; open it in Excel or any spreadsheet.
KUIPER: keys 1 to 1,000, radius =SQRT(k), angle =2*PI()*MOD(k*2654435769,4294967296)/4294967296, X and Y, and the chart.
TWO NUMBERS, BIG KEYS and INVESTIGATE: one dot worked by hand, where the short formula stops being exact, and three things to try.
