# See Through

See Through takes you from a field of stars down to any of 5,800 UK substations. The substation then rises in 3D on
the real satellite ground with its pylons, drawn by rule from open map data.

Open it here: https://ventusltd.github.io/kuiper-belt/see-through/

## How to play

1. Type a substation name and press GO.
2. Watch the fall from the stars to that substation.
3. Choose KUIPER, MAP, SAT or 3D to change the view.
4. Drag to orbit. Pinch, or use the mouse wheel, to zoom.
5. On a touch screen, push the joystick to fly. On a keyboard, use W A S D or the arrow keys.

## Run it from your own computer

Download the newest dated folder and open `index.html` in a web browser. The map, the satellite imagery and the
terrain come from online services, so it needs the internet.

## How the folders are organised

- The stable link above always opens the newest dated folder, `202610050609/`.
- Each folder is named by a date and time stamp: year, month, day, hour, minute.
- Earlier dated folders are kept unchanged as history, so old links keep working.
